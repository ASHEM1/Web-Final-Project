Common Greetings

こんにちは

Romaji: Konnichiwa

English: Hello / Good afternoon

Context: Standard polite greeting used during daylight hours.

ありがとうございます

Romaji: Arigatou gozaimasu

English: Thank you very much

Context: Polite and respectful. Use it with teachers and elders.

すみません

Romaji: Sumimasen

English: Excuse me / Sorry

Context: Incredibly versatile. Use it to get a waiter's attention.
