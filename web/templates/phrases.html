Essential Japanese Phrases

Boost your conversational skills with these fundamental everyday expressions.

Common Greetings

こんにちは

Romaji: Konnichiwa

English: Hello / Good afternoon

Context: Standard polite greeting used during daylight hours.

ありがとうございます

Romaji: Arigatou gozaimasu

English: Thank you very much

Context: Polite and respectful. Use it with teachers and elders.

すみません

Romaji: Sumimasen

English: Excuse me / Sorry

Context: Incredibly versatile. Use it to get a waiter's attention.

これは何ですか？

Romaji: kore wa nanji desu ka?

English: What is this?

Context: Useful for asking about objects or items.

それ是什么？

Romaji: sore wa nan desu ka?

English: What is that?

Context: Useful for asking about objects or items.

どういたしまして

Romaji: dou itashimashite

English: You're welcome

Context: Polite response to "Thank you."

どこ

Romaji: Doko

English: Where

Context: Used to indicate a location.

そこ

Romaji:Soko

English: There

Context: Refers to a location near the listener.

ここ

Romaji: Koko

English: Here

Context: Refers to a location near the speaker.

あそこ

Romaji: Asoko

English: Over there

Context: Refers to a location far from both speaker and listener.

いつ

Romaji: Itsu

English: When

Context: Used to ask about time or schedule.

なぜ

Romaji: Naze

English: Why

Context: Used to ask for reasons or explanations.

どうして

Romaji: Doushite

English: Why / How come

Context: Similar to "Naze" but can be more casual.
